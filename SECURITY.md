# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Send suspected security or privacy vulnerabilities to `security@phera-wedding.com`. Do not include guest data, credentials, access tokens, or other sensitive information in the initial report.

Include:

- The affected URL or feature
- Reproduction steps
- Expected and actual behavior
- The potential impact
- A safe proof of concept, if available

Phera will acknowledge reports as soon as reasonably possible, investigate in good faith, and coordinate remediation and disclosure when appropriate.

## Safe-harbor expectations

Researchers must avoid privacy violations, service disruption, social engineering, data destruction, automated high-volume testing, and accessing more data than needed to demonstrate the issue. Stop testing and report immediately if personal information is exposed.

This policy does not authorize testing of third-party services, including Google or Firebase.
