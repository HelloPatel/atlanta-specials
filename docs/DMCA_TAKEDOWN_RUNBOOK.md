# DMCA and content takedown runbook

## Before relying on DMCA safe harbor

1. Register the legal entity’s designated agent at <https://dmca.copyright.gov/osp/>.
2. Publish the same agent information on `/copyright`.
3. Renew the designation at least every three years and update changes promptly.
4. Maintain and reasonably implement a repeat-infringer policy.

## Notice intake

1. Timestamp and preserve the original notice.
2. Confirm it identifies the work, disputed material and URL, contact information, signature, good-faith statement, and perjury statement.
3. Do not promise removal until the notice is reviewed.
4. Escalate unclear, abusive, or high-value disputes to counsel.

## Takedown

1. Preserve a restricted evidentiary copy.
2. Disable access to the specific material, not unrelated account content.
3. Notify the uploader and provide the notice and counter-notice instructions.
4. Record the affected user, wedding, material, claimant, action, and repeat-infringer count.

## Counter-notice

1. Confirm the counter-notice includes the legally required identification, mistake statement, contact details, jurisdiction consent, service consent, and signature.
2. Forward a valid counter-notice to the claimant.
3. Restore the material only after the statutory waiting period if the claimant does not report filing a court action, after counsel confirms the timeline.

## Non-copyright complaints

Route privacy, harassment, impersonation, trademark, right-of-publicity, and safety complaints through a separate legal review. A DMCA form is not the correct process for every content dispute.
