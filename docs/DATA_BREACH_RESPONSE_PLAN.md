# Data breach response plan

## Roles

Assign named owners before launch:

- Incident commander
- Security and engineering lead
- Privacy and legal lead
- Communications lead
- Firebase and hosting administrator

Maintain offline contact details and escalation backups.

## Immediate response

1. Preserve evidence and start an incident log.
2. Contain the incident without destroying logs.
3. Rotate exposed credentials, revoke sessions, restrict rules, or disable affected features as needed.
4. Determine whether Firebase, email, domain, source control, or another provider is involved and open the appropriate vendor incident.
5. Do not make public claims before facts are verified.

## Investigation

Document:

- What happened and how it was detected
- Systems, collections, backups, and vendors affected
- Data categories, people, states, and countries affected
- Whether data was viewed, changed, exfiltrated, or unavailable
- Start and end times
- Containment and remediation

## Legal assessment

Counsel should determine whether the event meets each applicable definition of a security breach, who must be notified, required content, timing, regulator or credit-bureau notices, law-enforcement delay, and documentation duties.

Georgia law generally requires notice in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay. Do not use that statement as the only jurisdictional analysis.

## Notification and recovery

1. Use accurate, plain language and approved channels.
2. Give affected people practical protective steps.
3. Restore from verified clean systems and test access controls.
4. Monitor for recurrence and abuse.
5. Complete a post-incident review with assigned corrective actions and deadlines.
